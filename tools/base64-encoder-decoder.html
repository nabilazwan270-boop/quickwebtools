Base64 Encoder Decoder

Encode plain text into Base64, or decode Base64 back into readable text.

This tool supports Unicode text, so Indonesian text and emoji also work correctly.

How it works:
• Encode turns normal text into Base64 format.
• Decode turns Base64 back into readable text.
• Unicode characters are handled safely.
